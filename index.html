K&U Generations Hub Ltd. is a non-profit arts organization based in Calgary, Canada. Through music, theatre, dance, and creative arts, we provide a meaningful platform for children and youth to grow, express themselves, and discover their potential.

K&U Introduction

K&U Introduction

We believe every child has unique talent. The arts help children build confidence, develop creativity, strengthen communication skills, and learn teamwork and responsibility.

Our mission is to connect families and cultures through high-quality arts education and public benefit performances. We help children discover who they are on stage, grow through artistic practice, and shine within the community.

Core Team

Teachers

Anna Lupeko

Anna is a professional actress with a comprehensive theatrical education that spans drama theatre, puppet theatre, musical theatre, and more. This diverse background makes her a highly versatile professional who can work effectively with students of any age.

Anna is a founder and Artistic Director of Skylark Art School — a comprehensive art school for kids & teens. She also co-founded and served as the Managing Director of the Ukrainian theatre STRUM, where she acted and produced 4 theatre projects.

Anna has been teaching children and adults since 2019, bringing together over seven years of dedicated teaching practice.

For her classes, Anna uses her own teaching methodology — a unique blend of techniques from the Kharkiv Theatre School, along with her personal acting and teaching experience.

Anna also has a musical background in piano and vocal performance. She deeply believes in the extraordinary power of art — a power that reveals talent, shapes personality, and inspires growth.

Education: Master’s Degree in Performing Arts
